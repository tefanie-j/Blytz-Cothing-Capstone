The Problem

Designing Shouldn't Mean Endless Revisions.

Clients often have a clear idea in their head — but translating that idea into words for an artist is harder than it sounds. Small gaps in communication turn into rounds of costly revisions.

01

Hard to Visualize

Clients may know what they want but cannot clearly picture the final clothing design in their mind.

02

Miscommunication

Artists may interpret a client's description differently from what the client originally imagined.

03

Too Many Revisions

Small misunderstandings compound into multiple rounds of revisions and wasted time for everyone.

Visualize it first. Refine the idea. Then send it to the artist.

User Guide

From Idea to Visual in 4 Simple Steps

01

Describe Your Idea

Tell the platform what type of clothing you want and describe the style, colors, graphics, and placement.

02

Generate Your Mockup

The AI creates a visual mockup based on your description in a matter of minutes.

03

Review & Refine

Look at the generated concept and decide whether it represents your idea correctly.

04

Send It to Your Artist

Use the approved mockup as a visual reference when briefing your professional artist or designer.

Remember: the AI mockup is a visual reference, not the final production-ready artwork.
